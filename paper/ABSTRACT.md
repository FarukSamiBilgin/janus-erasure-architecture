# Extended abstract (UNPUBLISHED; manuscript in revision)

I propose a multi-qubit architecture for R-stacked polar Janus moiré bilayers (twisted WSSe and MoSSe), built on the companion result that electrons and holes co-localize on one moiré sublattice, spectrally separated from its mirror sublattice. The architecture maps four natural dualities of the platform onto processor roles: the two sublattices become data and ancilla; the dark and bright exciton manifolds become memory and gate rail; fast radiative decay versus long dark-state memory becomes erasure flag versus storage; and ferroelectric versus Stark control becomes configuration versus runtime.

Logical information lives in the dark valley–Kramers pair; two-qubit gates are dipolar-blockade CZ gates on the bright-exciton rail, whose dominant failure emits a photon and so becomes a located erasure. Error correction is staged: a passive envelope, a biased-erasure XZZX code, and an autonomous layer behind written go/no-go criteria.

All quantitative results of the manuscript depend on the companion paper, whose numbers are being recomputed; they are therefore omitted here. Per-site control is fatally limited by disorder at the system level (see github.com/FarukSamiBilgin/moire-300-logical-qubit-feasibility).
